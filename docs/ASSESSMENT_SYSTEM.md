# Assessment System

## What is measured
- Speaking duration and response-start speed
- Listening comprehension
- Reading comprehension
- Writing production
- Grammar production
- Grammar recognition
- Vocabulary retrieval
- Error recurrence
- Review retention

## Weekly test
Vocabulary recall, grammar production, listening, reading, writing and speaking. The report identifies strengths, weaknesses and next-week practice adjustments.

## CEFR gates
A2, B1, B2 and C1 checkpoints must include productive speaking and writing. Finishing lessons is not equivalent to reaching a CEFR level.

## Day 14 gate
The learner attempts 30–45 seconds of spontaneous speech with present, past and future reference. Low production accuracy triggers targeted remediation rather than a full restart.
