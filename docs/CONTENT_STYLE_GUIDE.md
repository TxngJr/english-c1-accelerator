# Content Style Guide

## Tone
Supportive, precise and adult. Never use children's-app styling or exaggerated praise for incorrect work.

## Thai support
- A1/A2: Thai explanations can be prominent
- B1: reduce Thai support
- B2: mostly English
- C1: nearly all English

## Vocabulary
Teach chunks and collocations whenever possible. Distinguish grammatical, natural, unnatural, formal and informal usage when relevant.

## Grammar
Short explanations; extensive contextualized production.

## Personalization
Frequently use programming, AI, websites, apps, startups, gaming, manga/anime, university, presentations, travel and career development without making every lesson about technology.
