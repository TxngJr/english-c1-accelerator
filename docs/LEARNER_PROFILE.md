# Learner Profile

## Target
CEFR C1 for university, professional work, programming/IT, technical documentation, presentations, international communication, media, gaming, future IELTS/TOEFL and travel.

## Priority order
1. Speaking
2. Listening
3. Reading
4. Writing
5. Grammar
6. Vocabulary
7. Pronunciation

## Initial estimate
- Overall practical English: A1+ to early A2
- Speaking production: A1
- Writing production: A1+
- Active grammar: A1+ to A2-
- Passive grammar recognition: A2 with isolated B1 knowledge
- Vocabulary: A1+ to A2
- Reading: A1+ to A2
- Listening: A1–A2
- Pronunciation: pending diagnosis

## Core learning problem
The learner recognizes more English than can be produced automatically. The system therefore prioritizes retrieval and production over repeated rule explanation.

## Known error targets
- `I am go to school.` → `I go to school.`
- `I do my homework yesterday.` → `I did my homework yesterday.`
- `I interests about reads manga.` → `I'm interested in reading manga.`
- `I try to learing an english.` → `I'm learning English.` / `I'm trying to improve my English.`
- `I do a programing language.` → `I practice programming.` / `I write code.`

## Study load
2–4 structured hours per day, seven days per week, plus optional immersion. Advancement depends on mastery rather than elapsed time.
