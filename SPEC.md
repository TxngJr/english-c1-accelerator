Every normal learning day should contain these components.

Suggested total:

2–4 hours.

5–10 minutes.

10–20 minutes.

## 4. Listening

20–40 minutes.

- connected speech
- contractions
- stress
- linking

### Final

Listen again without transcript.

Difficulty gradually increases.
